6+ years building products for startups and fintechs in the US, Brazil and Ecuador, mostly in remote, international teams.

- **Ótmow (Brazilian fintech):** put LLM agents to work on credit analysis — KYC, credit and receivables evaluations that run on their own as each deal moves through the pipeline — and built the KYC engine and the financial core of operations.
- **Shuffle (US):** part of the team that built the first version of the product, then led its engineering end to end as Engineer Lead — a Turborepo monorepo with 8 Next.js apps on a shared tRPC API.
- **Kushki:** payment flows, reconciliation and refunds in production.

Most of my work lives in private company repositories — the full CV is at **[christianchunga.com](https://www.christianchunga.com)**.
